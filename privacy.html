Privacy Policy — SHIVAM PIXA

Last updated: 3 November 2026

SHIVAM PIXA is an offline photo editor and collage maker that runs entirely in your browser. It offers an optional "Cloud Sync" feature that connects to your own Google Drive so your photos can be backed up and synced between your own devices.

What data the app accesses

  • Your Google account email address and basic profile (to show which account is signed in).
  • Photos you choose to import or edit, and the photo library you create in the app.
  • With your permission, the app uses the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which allows it to see, create and modify only the files it creates itself inside a dedicated "SHIVAM PIXA" folder in your own Google Drive. The app cannot read or change any other files in your Drive.

Where your data is stored

  • Local use: photos and edits stay on your device (browser storage). Nothing is uploaded.
  • Cloud Sync: photos and the library manifest are stored in your own Google Drive, inside the app-created "SHIVAM PIXA" folder. Google's servers store that data under your account, exactly like any file you save yourself.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, sell, share or analyse your photos or personal data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or for training AI models.
  • We do not transfer your data to any third party. There is no backend server — the app talks directly to Google's APIs with the access token you grant.
  • We have no access to your Drive files beyond the app's own folder, and no access after you sign out or revoke permission.

Data retention and deletion

Your photos remain in your Google Drive until you delete them — inside the app (delete a photo while signed in) or directly in Drive (delete the "SHIVAM PIXA" folder). Clearing the app's local library or uninstalling/clearing site data removes all locally stored edits and settings. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account page (myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party apps with account access).
